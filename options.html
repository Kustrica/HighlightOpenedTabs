Options

Site access is required

To highlight links when you hover them, the extension needs access to web pages.

You can continue without this — tab-bar features stay available, but on-page link highlighting will not work.

Click “Grant access” above and allow in the dialog, or open about:addons → Highlight Opened Tabs → Permissions and enable “Access your data for all websites”.

Tab selection

Shows a number on the extension icon while you hover a link, and briefly after you switch to a tab.

Other open copies are briefly selected in the tab bar (multi-select style).

Matching tabs in the tab bar are highlighted while the cursor is on the link. Works alongside the on-page highlight below.

Turn off if highlights should stay while you move the cursor to the tab bar or side panel.
